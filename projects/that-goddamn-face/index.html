UJI Game Jam 2026 · Winner

That Goddamn Face!

A first-person game made for the UJI Game Jam 2026, whose theme was “One enemy only”. You fight the same man across three eras: a prehistoric cave, a medieval castle and a Wild West saloon.

My role
3D characters and animation
Time
48 hours
Team
6 people
Built with
Unity, Blender, Mixamo
Result
Winner of the jam

One enemy, three eras

The theme asked for a single enemy, so we made him the whole game: he keeps coming back through history, and so do you. I modelled every version of him in Blender, each one with that same unmistakable moustache, and adapted Mixamo animations to fit the game.

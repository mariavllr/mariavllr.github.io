PhD project

Unity · C# · Blender · 2025 – now

WFC world generator

A 3D tile-based world generator inspired by Townscaper, with two modes: press a button to generate a whole island, or build it yourself by dragging tiles from a queue while the world collapses around your choices. I model the tiles in Blender and write the solver, tools and shaders in Unity.

Research

what I work on every day

PhD · GameRS, Universitat Jaume I · 2025 – now

Interactive, artist-oriented procedural worlds

My thesis studies procedural content generation with Wave Function Collapse. I look at how generated worlds can keep the feel of hand-made art, and how artists and players can shape what the algorithm builds.

About me

hi again!

Hi! I'm María and I live in Spain. I studied a degree in Game Design and Development and specialised in 3D. My favourite games are The Legend of Zelda series, Animal Crossing and Stardew Valley. I'm a creative person, open to new ideas. I love making new things and always give the art side a lot of weight, which is where the idea for my thesis came from. Besides games, my hobbies are drawing, playing the piano and making clay figures.

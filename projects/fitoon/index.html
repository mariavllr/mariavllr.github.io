Fitoon

An obstacle-race game for running on a treadmill. No controller, no wearables: just the phone in front of you and your own head.

My role
AR programming, 3D characters, UI art
Team
GameRS Videogames Research Group
Built with
Unity, C#, ARCore face tracking, Blender
Platform
Android

The challenge

Running on a treadmill gets boring fast, and the apps made for it are mostly simulators. Fitness games like Ring Fit Adventure need extra hardware. We wanted a game anyone could play with only a phone, inspired by party racers like Fall Guys.

Run faster and your character runs faster. Look to the side and it moves that way. It even works if you run on the spot without a treadmill.

My work

  • Developed the AR face-tracking controller, including the cadence and direction estimation.
  • Designed and modelled every character in Blender, in a low-poly style built for mobile.
  • Built the character creator: skins, colours and running shoes.
  • Drew the UI art.
  • The hardest problem: the FFT cadence. Noise in the head-tracking signal could give wrong step frequencies, so we ended up looking for ways to smooth it.
  • Bots trained with machine learning to race alongside the player.
  • A local multiplayer version with peer-to-peer over Wi-Fi. Latency was too high, so for now we decided to keep going with bots.
  • Gym integration, medals and rankings, either global or per gym.
